3.5 Traditional Time Conventions in BaZi & Architectural Choice
Confirmed Source Fact vs. Astrological Interpretation:
Source Fact: The existing codebase alternates between uncorrected civil integer hours in local clock time (chinese.ts) and Greenwich UTC clock time (baziEngine.ts). Neither path applies solar time corrections.
Traditional Astrological Conventions:
True Solar Time / Local Apparent Solar Time (LAST, 真太阳时):
Classical Chinese timekeeping was sundial-based (日晷). Sundials directly measure Local Apparent Solar Time, where solar noon (Wu hour, 午时) occurs at the Sun's upper meridian transit. LAST applies both geographic longitude adjustment (

Local Mean Solar Time (LMST, 平太阳时):
Applies only the geographic longitude adjustment (
), omitting the Equation of Time on the premise that double-hours are uniform intervals of mean time.
Civil Standard Time (北京时间 / Standard Clock Time):
Uses the civil standard clock time of the administrative timezone without geographic adjustment. Common in casual software, but results in severe errors for locations far from standard meridians (e.g., Xinjiang vs. Beijing).
Zi Hour Policy (子时):
Early/Late Zi (早晚子时): Late Zi (23:00–24:00) belongs to the current calendar day's Day Pillar with the next day's Zi hour stem. Early Zi (00:00–01:00) belongs to the new day's Day Pillar.
Unified Zi (不分早晚子时): The entire 23:00–01:00 window belongs to the next day's Day Pillar.
